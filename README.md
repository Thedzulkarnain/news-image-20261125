A one-page newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in 35 stories: Pakistan's first astronaut flies to Tiangong, Asia's chip stocks crash on AI doubts, and two tankers hit mines and burn in Hormuz. Built in the "The World in One Day" format — a UTC timeline, a six-number stats strip, three illustrated leads, ten medium stories with drawn thumbnails, a world map with numbered pins, a 22-item in-brief list, a DATE CHECK box excluding earlier news, and a HOW SURE verification legend (OFFICIAL / CONFIRMED / REPORTED / CLAIM). All text is code-rendered from HTML/CSS; the three lead illustrations are original flat drawings. `main.ts` copies `art.png` to `/output/entry.png` after verifying byte identity and the 1024x1024 dimensions.
